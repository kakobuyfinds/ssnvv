How to use the OrientDig spreadsheet

Find the seller link and the exact option

Open the HipobuyFind listing and look for the original seller link. If you plan to buy through OrientDig, check whether it accepts that link and offers the size and color you want. Save the item number with your selection so you can compare it later.

Compare measurements and warehouse QC photos

For sneakers, check what the seller measures: foot length and insole length are different. For hoodies and jackets, measure the chest width, body length and sleeves of a garment you own. When warehouse photos are available, match the item’s label, color and details to your order before approving shipment. Ask for a measurement photo if fit is uncertain.

Estimate the delivered cost

Add the item price, any domestic delivery and service fees, and estimated international shipping. Check which taxes or duties apply at your destination. For bulky items like puffer jackets, check whether the shipping line uses volumetric weight and how it rounds the billable weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a directory-style shopping guide for comparing product listings and collecting seller links before placing an order through a shopping agent. The listings on this page come from HipobuyFind.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check the specific listing and the shopping agent’s warehouse order page for available photos. For fit and color checks, look for photos tied to the exact option you ordered.

Do the prices include shipping?

The table shows reference item prices only. Review the seller price and get a delivery estimate using your destination before paying.
